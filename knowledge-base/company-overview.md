# Auralis — Company Overview

Auralis is a fictional consumer audio company that designs and sells wireless headphones and accessories.

## Customer Support

Support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 UTC.

Customer support can help with:
- Product information
- Orders and shipping
- Returns and refunds
- Warranty and replacements
- Account issues
- Troubleshooting

## Products

Auralis currently sells:

- Auralis One — wireless over-ear headphones
- Auralis One Mini — compact wireless headphones
- Auralis Buds — true wireless earbuds
- Auralis Travel Case — protective carrying case

## Important

Support agents should not promise refunds, replacements, or other exceptions unless the relevant policy or customer-specific information supports the decision.
