# Auralis Product Guide

## Auralis One

Premium wireless over-ear headphones.

Key features:
- Active noise cancellation
- Bluetooth connectivity
- USB-C charging
- Up to 30 hours of battery life with ANC disabled

## Auralis One Mini

Compact wireless over-ear headphones.

Key features:
- Active noise cancellation
- Bluetooth connectivity
- USB-C charging
- Up to 24 hours of battery life with ANC disabled

## Auralis Buds

True wireless earbuds.

Key features:
- Bluetooth connectivity
- Charging case
- Touch controls
- Up to 8 hours of listening time per charge

## Model Identification

The product model can usually be found on the product label, packaging, or the original order information.
