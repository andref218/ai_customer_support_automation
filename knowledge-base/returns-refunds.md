# Auralis Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard Returns

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Returned products should be in reasonable condition and include the original accessories supplied with the product.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued after the returned product has been received and inspected.

Approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method.

## Non-Returnable Situations

The following may require additional review:

- Requests submitted after the 30-day return period
- Products with significant customer-caused damage
- Missing essential components
- Requests involving promotional or replacement units

## Exchanges

Customers who receive a defective product may be eligible for a replacement under the warranty policy.

Return eligibility and warranty eligibility are separate decisions.

## Important

Do not promise a refund before the relevant eligibility conditions have been verified.
