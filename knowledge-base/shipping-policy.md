# Auralis Shipping Policy

## Processing

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days.

## Standard Delivery

Estimated delivery time for standard shipping is 3–5 business days after dispatch.

## Tracking

A tracking number is normally provided after an order has been dispatched.

## Delayed Orders

If an order is later than the estimated delivery window, support should verify the order and tracking information before determining the next step.

## Lost Packages

A package should not be considered lost solely because it is delayed. Support may need to review the tracking history and carrier status.

## Required Customer Information

For order-related investigation, support may need:

- Order number
- Delivery address or other verification information
- Tracking number, if available
