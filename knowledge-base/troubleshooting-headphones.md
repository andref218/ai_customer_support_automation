# Auralis Headphones Troubleshooting Guide

## Headphones Do Not Turn On

1. Charge the headphones for at least 30 minutes.
2. Try a different USB-C cable or charging adapter.
3. Attempt to power the headphones on again.

## Bluetooth Connection Problems

1. Confirm Bluetooth is enabled on the device.
2. Move the headphones close to the device.
3. Remove the headphones from the device's Bluetooth settings.
4. Restart the headphones.
5. Pair the headphones again.

## No Audio

1. Confirm the headphones are connected to the intended device.
2. Check the device volume.
3. Check whether another audio output is selected.
4. Restart the headphones and reconnect.

## Issue Persists

If the issue continues after the relevant troubleshooting steps, support may need the product model, order details, and a description of the failure before determining whether the issue is covered by warranty.
