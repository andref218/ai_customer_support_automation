# Auralis Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty

Auralis products include a 24-month limited warranty starting from the original purchase date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures that occur during normal use.

## Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not cover:

- Accidental damage
- Water or liquid damage
- Cosmetic damage
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage caused by unauthorized modifications
- Damage caused by misuse

## Replacement

A replacement may be offered when:

1. The product is within the warranty period.
2. The reported issue is covered by the warranty.
3. Troubleshooting does not resolve the issue.
4. The purchase can be verified.

A replacement is not automatically guaranteed solely because a product has stopped working.

## Required Information

Support may need:

- Order number or proof of purchase
- Product model
- Description of the problem
- Relevant troubleshooting already attempted

## Escalation

Cases involving exceptions to this policy should be reviewed by a human support agent.
